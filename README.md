\# AI Skills



A growing collection of reusable Agent Skills.



一个持续收集和维护可复用 Agent Skills 的仓库。



\## Skills / 已有 Skills



\### paper-reading



A concise research-paper reading mentor for computer science and AI papers.



一个面向计算机科学和人工智能论文的精读导师 Skill。



It helps with:



\- Formula and model-module explanations

\- Consistent numerical examples

\- Symbol reminders

\- Paper reproduction, Git, and environment guidance

\- Clear, concise teaching for beginners



它可以帮助用户：



\- 理解论文公式和模型模块

\- 使用前后一致的数值例子讲解抽象概念

\- 提醒容易遗忘的符号含义

\- 指导论文复现、Git 和环境配置

\- 用简洁、清晰且适合初学者的方式解释问题



\### literature-review

An evidence-grounded research-paper discovery and literature-review Skill for computer science and artificial intelligence.

一个面向计算机科学和人工智能研究的论文检索与文献综述 Skill，强调来源核验、证据追踪和公平比较。

It helps with:

\- Defining research scope, time ranges, venue coverage, and review depth
\- Searching top conferences, top journals, and other reliable scholarly sources
\- Screening, deduplicating, and verifying papers, publication status, and links
\- Producing structured paper lists with direct paper and code links
\- Extracting research problems, innovations, methods, datasets, metrics, results, and limitations
\- Comparing only compatible experimental settings while retaining relevant secondary papers
\- Writing literature reviews in the structure requested by the user
\- Qualifying current-best results with datasets, metrics, settings, dates, and verification levels

它可以帮助用户：

\- 明确研究主题、时间范围、venue 范围和综述深度
\- 从顶级会议、顶级期刊及其他可靠学术来源检索论文
\- 筛选、去重并核验论文、发表状态和链接
\- 生成带有论文及代码直达链接的结构化论文列表
\- 提取研究问题、创新点、方法、数据集、评价指标、实验结果和局限性
\- 只对实验设置兼容的结果进行直接比较，同时保留方向相关的次要论文
\- 严格按照用户指定的结构撰写文献综述
\- 使用数据集、指标、实验设置、截止日期和核验等级限定当前最佳结果
